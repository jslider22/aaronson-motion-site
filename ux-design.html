D2M

D2M, short for Design to Motion, is a play on Motion Design. It's a workout app I designed and built for my own training. It acts as a guide: it tells you what's up today, walks you through each set, and keeps track of your progress so you don't have to. It's an ongoing project: I keep refining it as I use it each week, with an eye toward a wider release.

Every screen shares one cohesive look, with blue for action, mint for progress and gold for friends, so the app stays calm and easy to read mid-workout.

Testing

I test D2M the same way I'd use it: at the gym, between sets. I keep notes on anything that slows me down or feels unclear, then fix it that week. Friends tell me what they'd want the app to do for them, and I turn those ideas into new features, like logging a weighted vest on walks, or keep them on the list for later. Most of what's in this case study started as one of those notes or requests.

Key Screens

Features

The details that make it a guide: logging any sport, timed sets, an extensive exercise library with how-to sheets and demo videos, stretch ideas, and quick links to your music, podcasts and videos.

Friends

Light accountability: friends see only each other's weekly goal progress and streak, can cheer anyone who hits their goal, and one switch turns sharing off. Gold marks everything friends-related.

User Flows

From first-run setup to booking a session, finishing a workout and swapping exercises around an injury.

Motion System

Small animations reward progress without getting in the way: a water reminder, the level bar filling and brightening, a level-up, the workout-complete check, the weekly goal celebration and the Today tab bolt.

Logo

The D>M mark puts a forward chevron between the D and the M: design moving into motion. Logo designed by Jonny Aaronson.

Style Guide

A tight color and type system, a shared set of components, and a dark and a light mode.

Icons

Sport icons for logging cardio and activities, designed or adapted for the app.

SkiPS

SkiPS is a mobile app that helps skiers find their friends at the mountain. The app also allows users to explore the trails maps and terrain of their favorite mountains.

Try out the app!

  • Look around the trail map of the nearest mountain and view the key stats.
  • Checkin on where your friends are and get in touch with them to meet up.
  • View your profile and update your settings and preferences.
  • Add to your contacts by sending invites to join you on the app.
